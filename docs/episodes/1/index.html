Donato’s local AI journey

Usman Pirzada from AMD interviews me about Strix Halo, Toolboxes and the local AI community.

Notes & chapters

I talk about how experimenting with Strix Halo led to Toolboxes, repeatable benchmarks and a community building specialised inference engines. We discuss Vulkan, ROCm, llama.cpp, Helogen, Gufo and ROCmFPX; models from Qwen, DeepSeek and GLM; and what comes next for local AI.
